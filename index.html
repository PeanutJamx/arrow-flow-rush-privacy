Privacy Policy for Arrow Flow Rush

Last updated: September 29, 2026

CGN Games ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Arrow Flow Rush.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Arrow Flow Rush.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Flow Rush does not require users to create an account, sign in, or directly provide personal information such as a name, phone number, postal address, or date of birth.

Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on the user's device to provide features such as saving level progress, lives, and game settings.

2. Advertising

The current version of Arrow Flow Rush does not display advertisements.

If advertising features are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated to reflect those changes.

3. Third-Party Services

Arrow Flow Rush is developed using Unity. The application may include third-party software components or SDKs required for application functionality and distribution.

Where applicable, third-party services may process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We review our application's data practices and Google Play disclosures when third-party services are added, removed, or changed.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Any data processing performed by third-party services, where applicable, is subject to the privacy practices of those service providers.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the application. Game progress and preferences stored locally remain on the user's device unless functionality requiring external processing is introduced.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game progress and preferences remain on the user's device and may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

Arrow Flow Rush currently does not provide user accounts and therefore does not maintain user account data that requires an account-deletion process.

7. Children's Privacy

Arrow Flow Rush does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. The game's intended audience and applicable age classifications are declared separately through Google Play.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, third-party services, or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page together with an updated revision date.
